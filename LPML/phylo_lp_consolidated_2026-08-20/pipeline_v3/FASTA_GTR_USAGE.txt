Whole alignment, JC69:
  python phylo_lp_runner.py alignment.fa --out-prefix run_jc --top-k 40

Random sample of at most 500 columns:
  python phylo_lp_runner.py alignment.fa --out-prefix run_sample --max-columns 500 --seed 17

GTR+F (exchangeabilities AC,AG,AT,CG,CT,GT; +F from selected columns):
  python phylo_lp_runner.py alignment.fa --out-prefix run_gtr --model gtr+f \
    --gtr-rates 1.0,2.1,0.7,0.8,1.4,0.9 --top-k 40

Branch-effect LP default:
  --branch-relaxation auto
    JC-like model -> legacy one-mode/two-point representation
    non-JC reversible model -> full-range 3-mode spectral relaxation

  Non-JC spectral tuning:
    --spectral-support-points 7     # default; >=5, larger=tighter/slower preprocessing

  Diagnostic fallback:
    --branch-relaxation two-point

Outputs:
  <prefix>.summary.json
  <prefix>.timings.json
  <prefix>.topk.tsv
  <prefix>.best.tree
  <prefix>.sampled.fa   (only when sampling actually reduces the alignment)

Final integral-tree branch lengths:
  Defaults to --branch-min 1e-12, --branch-max auto, and --branch-multistart 4.
  Auto chooses a model-adaptive saturation length from Q's spectral gap.  The multistart
  avoids a saturated-gradient failure if a fractional LP branch effect is weakly identified.

Duplicate columns:
  Identical across-taxon site patterns are stored once and weighted by their multiplicity.
  The summary JSON reports exact and LP unique-pattern counts.
